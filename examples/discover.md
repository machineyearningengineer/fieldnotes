Polyanionic cathodes are attractive for stationary storage due to intrinsic safety characteristics that simplify system design, alongside reduced susceptibility to slurry gelation during processing\. [3](https://doi.org/10.3390/batteries12080303)

Early deployment of sodium-ion technology in cold-climate stationary storage is supported by its demonstrated low-temperature performance advantage\. [3](https://doi.org/10.3390/batteries12080303)

## Application Evaluation and Practical Limitations

Full-cell competitiveness for grid storage requires balancing electrode capacities, first-cycle losses, voltage efficiency, interfacial stability, and manufacturing scale\. [1](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/chemistry/articles/10.3389/fchem.2026.2007977/full)

Intrinsically low sodium-ion diffusivity in cathode materials causes voltage polarization and restricts high-rate performance, presenting ongoing operational challenges\. [4](https://chemrxiv.org/doi/full/10.26434/chemrxiv.15005593/v1)
